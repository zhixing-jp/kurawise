Privacy Policy

KuraPhoto (macOS app) / Effective and last updated: October 18, 2026

Kurawise (“we”) respects the privacy of users of the macOS app “KuraPhoto” (the “App”) and handles information as follows.

1. Summary

  • The App does not send or collect your personal information, photos, videos, or usage data to any server of ours. We do not operate any such server.
  • Reading, organizing, duplicate checking, and missing-files checking of photos and videos all happen only on your Mac.
  • We use no advertising, tracking, or third-party analytics.
  • The only information we receive is the content of emails you choose to send us (Section 3).

2. Information the App handles and why

InformationPurposeWhere it is stored / sent
Photos and videos in folders or devices you choose (iPhone, SD card, etc.) and their metadata, such as date taken and locationOrganizing into folders by date, detecting already imported items, duplicate checks, missing-files checksOnly on your Mac. Never sent externally
The Photos app library (only if you allow access)Extracting original photos and videos into folders. Photos in the library are never changed or deletedOnly on your Mac. If an original is only in iCloud, macOS Photos downloads it from Apple’s iCloud
Location where photos were taken (latitude and longitude)Only when you turn on “Also add the place name to folder names”, to look up place namesLatitude and longitude are sent to Apple’s map service (a standard macOS feature) to get place names. Nothing is sent to us
Settings, last destination, and processing records (logs, undo records, duplicate-check analysis results, records of extracted file sizes, dates taken and locations already read, place names looked up)To reuse your settings, resume after interruptions, enable “Undo”, and speed up processingOn your Mac (app settings, inside the destination folder, the App’s own storage area)

3. Information we receive when you contact us

If you email us, we receive your email address, the message, and any attached files (such as logs). We use them only to reply and to investigate issues, and we do not share them with third parties. After your inquiry is resolved, we delete them once they are no longer needed. You may ask us to delete them at any time.

4. Data retention and deletion

All data the App handles stays on your Mac; we do not hold it. You can delete it yourself at any time:

  • App settings and records: move the App to the Trash, then delete the “com.kurawise.kuraphoto” folder in “Library › Containers”.
  • Records in the destination: delete the “KuraPhoto Logs” folder and the hidden “Undo” folders (names starting with “.KuraPhoto-”) in your destination folder. After deletion, earlier organizing can no longer be undone. Logs older than 3 years are deleted automatically during the next operation.
  • Permissions such as Photos library access: revoke them at any time in macOS “System Settings › Privacy & Security”.

5. Sharing with third parties

We do not share or sell your information to third parties. Place-name lookup (if you enable it) and downloading originals from iCloud are performed through standard macOS features provided by Apple and are governed by Apple’s Privacy Policy.

6. In-app purchases

Purchases of the App and in-app purchases are processed by Apple’s App Store. We never receive your name, payment information, or Apple Account information.

7. Crash reports

Only if you allow “Share with app developers” in macOS settings, anonymous crash reports may be provided to us through Apple. We use them solely to fix bugs. You can turn this off at any time in macOS “System Settings › Privacy & Security › Analytics & Improvements”.

8. Permissions

  • Photos library: requested only when you use “Import from Photos Library”. Read-only; nothing is changed or deleted.
  • Files and folders: the App accesses only the folders and files you choose in a panel.
  • USB devices: used to import photos from iPhones, cameras, and similar devices.
  • Notifications: used to let you know when processing finishes (the App works without this permission).

9. Children’s privacy

The App does not collect personal information from anyone, regardless of age.

10. Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will announce it on this page along with the update date.
